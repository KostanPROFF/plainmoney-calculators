Quick conversion rule

For a full-time job of 40 hours a week and 52 paid weeks, a year has 2,080 work hours. Divide a salary by 2,080 to get the hourly rate, or multiply an hourly wage by 2,080 for the annual salary. A shortcut: double the hourly rate and add three zeros. $25 an hour is about $50,000 a year.
